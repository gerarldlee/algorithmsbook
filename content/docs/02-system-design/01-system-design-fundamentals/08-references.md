## Books

- *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* by Martin Kleppmann — data systems, replication, partitioning, transactions, and stream processing.
- *Site Reliability Engineering* by Betsy Beyer, Chris Jones, Jennifer Petoff, and Niall Richard Murphy — service-level objectives, monitoring, capacity, and operational reliability.
- *Building Secure and Reliable Systems* by Charbel Aridi, Jessica Twombly, and John Ward — secure design patterns, dependency risk, and operational controls.
- *Network Security: Private Communication in a Public World* by Ross Anderson — transport security, authentication, and protocol design.

## Websites

- [Google SRE Workbook: Service Level Objectives](https://sre.google/workbook/service-level-objectives/) — SLI eligibility, SLO design, and error budgets.
- [RFC 9110: HTTP Semantics](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110) and [RFC 9114: HTTP/3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9114) — HTTP methods, status semantics, and HTTP/3.
- [RFC 9000: QUIC](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9000) and [RFC 6455: The WebSocket Protocol](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6455) — transport and full-duplex protocol mechanics.
- [gRPC documentation](https://grpc.io/docs/what-is-grpc/core-concepts/) — RPC calls, deadlines, metadata, status codes, and streaming.
- [Envoy load balancing documentation](https://www.envoyproxy.io/docs/envoy/latest/intro/arch_overview/upstream/load_balancing) — health, balancing policies, and traffic distribution.
- [Traefik dynamic configuration](https://doc.traefik.io/traefik/reference/dynamic-configuration/) — routers, services, entry points, and TLS configuration.
- [tRPC documentation](https://trpc.io/docs/) — TypeScript procedure definitions, routers, clients, and validation.
- [OpenAPI specification](https://spec.openapis.org/oas/latest.html) and [AsyncAPI specification](https://www.asyncapi.com/docs/reference/specification/latest) — HTTP and event-driven contract descriptions.
- [AWS Key Management Service best practices](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/kms/latest/developerguide/best-practices.html) — key policies, grants, rotation, and separation of duties.
- [OWASP Top 10](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-ten/) and [OWASP Cheat Sheet Series](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/) — application-security risks and defensive controls.
- [SLSA specification](https://slsa.dev/spec/v1.1/) and [GitHub secure use reference](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/security-for-github-actions/security-guides/security-hardening-for-github-actions) — build provenance and CI supply-chain controls.
- [Sigstore Cosign documentation](https://docs.sigstore.dev/cosign/) — image signing, verification, and attestations.

## Books

- *Clean Code: A Handbook of Agile Software Craftsmanship* by Robert C. Martin introduces responsibility, naming, and behavior-preserving design principles.
- *Refactoring: Improving the Design of Existing Code* by Martin Fowler explains the catalog of code smells and refactoring techniques.
- *Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software* by Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson, and John Vlissides defines the classic creational, structural, and behavioral patterns.
- *Effective Java* by Joshua Bloch provides practical guidance for encapsulation, APIs, collections, and maintenance in an object-oriented language.

## Websites

- [The Refactoring Catalog](https://refactoring.com/catalog/) organizes common smells and the transformations that commonly address them.
- [Refactoring Guru Design Patterns](https://refactoring.guru/design-patterns/overview) provides visual explanations, use cases, and trade-offs for classic patterns.
- [Martin Fowler's Patterns Catalog](https://martinfowler.com/eaaCatalog/) describes enterprise architecture patterns and their forces.
- [Technical Debt](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/TechnicalDebt.html) explains why shortcuts create future work and why deliberate debt can sometimes be rational.

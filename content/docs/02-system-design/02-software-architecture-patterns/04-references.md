## Books

- Sam Newman, *Building Microservices: Designing Fine-Grained Systems*, 2nd edition, O'Reilly, 2021.
- Chris Richardson, *Microservices Patterns: A Guide to Designing and Rewriting Distributed Applications*, 2nd edition, Microsoft Press, 2023.
- Eric Evans, *Domain-Driven Design: Tackling Complexity in the Heart of Software*, Addison-Wesley, 2004.
- Martin Kleppmann, *Designing Data-Intensive Applications: The Big Ideas Behind Reliable, Resilient, and Maintainable Systems*, O'Reilly, 2017.
- Michael T. Nygard, *Release It!: Design and Deploy Production-Ready Software*, 2nd edition, Pragmatic Bookshelf, 2018.

## Websites

- [Microservices.io Pattern Catalog](https://microservices.io/patterns/) — architectural patterns, including the transactional outbox, circuit breaker, and bulkhead.
- [Martin Fowler: Monolith First](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/MonolithFirst.html) — the case for starting with a modular monolith and extracting boundaries deliberately.
- [Martin Fowler: Micro Frontends](https://martinfowler.com/articles/micro-frontends.html) — deployment of a dedicated backend-for-frontend with each micro frontend.
- [Martin Fowler: StranglerFigApplication](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/StranglerFigApplication.html) — incremental replacement of legacy behavior through a routing boundary.
- [Istio Data Plane Modes](https://istio.io/latest/docs/overview/dataplane-modes/) — sidecar and ambient service-mesh data planes.
- [Linkerd Documentation](https://linkerd.io/docs/) — service-mesh architecture and proxy behavior.
- [Microsoft Azure Architecture Center: Circuit Breaker Pattern](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/patterns/circuit-breaker) and [Bulkhead Pattern](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/patterns/bulkhead) — failure isolation and overload controls.
- [Google Site Reliability Engineering Book](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/) — production guidance for latency, capacity, overload, and failure response.

## Books

- Martin Kleppmann, *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*, O'Reilly Media, chapters on transactions, replication, consistency, and stream processing.
- David Harris, *Digital Markets and Exchange Platforms*, Oxford University Press, market structure and matching-engine concepts.
- Larry Harris, *Trading and Exchanges: Market Microstructure in Practice*, Oxford University Press, order flow, liquidity, and trading-system design.
- Moorad Choudhry, *An Introduction to Banking*, second edition, Wiley, payment flows, operational risk, and banking architecture.
- Chris Richardson, *Learning Apache Kafka*, O'Reilly Media, durable event processing and exactly-once effects.
- Martin Fowler, *Patterns of Enterprise Application Architecture*, Addison-Wesley, transaction boundaries, persistence, and integration patterns.

## Websites

- [Apache Kafka](https://kafka.apache.org/) — event logs, consumer groups, delivery semantics, and transactional processing documentation.
- [PostgreSQL documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/) — isolation levels, transactional DDL, constraints, and replication behavior.
- [Redis sorted sets](https://redis.io/docs/latest/data-types/sorted-sets/) — ordered-set structures used for limited projections and queues.
- [IETF Web Payments](https://www.w3.org/TR/payment-request/) — browser payment request and payment method concepts.
- [ISO 20022](https://www.iso20022.org/) — financial messaging standards and implementation resources.
- [The Clearing House](https://www.theclearinghouse.org/) — ACH and RTP operating information.
- [FIX Trading Community](https://www.fixtrading.org/) — financial market connectivity standards and session protocols.
- [OASIS Financial Information eXchange](https://www.oasis-open.org/standards/fx) — financial information exchange standards.
- [PCI Security Standards Council](https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/) — payment security standards and assessment resources.

- Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission. *Internal Control—Integrated Framework*. COSO, 2013.
- European Banking Authority. *Guidelines on the Application of the Payment Services Regulation*. European Union, 2018.
- International Organization for Standardization. *ISO 31000:2018 Risk Management—Guidelines*. ISO, 2018.

- UK Information Commissioner's Office. *Guide to the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)*. ICO.

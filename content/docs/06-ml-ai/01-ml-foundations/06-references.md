## Books

- Kevin P. Murphy, *Machine Learning: A Probabilistic Perspective* — supervised learning, linear models, SVMs, neural networks, clustering, and PCA.
- Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani, and Jerome Friedman, *The Elements of Statistical Learning* — regularized regression, classification, ensembles, boosting, and dimensionality reduction.
- Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville, *Deep Learning* — neural-network mechanics, CNNs, recurrent networks, optimization, and representation learning.
- Simon J. D. Prince, *Understanding Deep Learning* — attention, Transformers, normalization, and modern training methods.
- Richard Szeliski, *Computer Vision: Algorithms and Applications* — visual feature extraction, video processing, object detection, and tracking.

## Websites

- [Scikit-learn supervised learning guide](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/supervised_learning.html) — linear models, SVMs, nearest neighbors, trees, forests, and gradient boosting.
- [Scikit-learn unsupervised learning guide](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/unsupervised_learning.html) — clustering, dimensionality reduction, density estimation, and anomaly detection.
- [Scikit-learn neural-network models](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/neural_networks.html) — multilayer perceptrons and related estimators.
- [XGBoost documentation](https://xgboost.readthedocs.io/en/stable/) — regularized gradient-boosted trees.
- [LightGBM documentation](https://lightgbm.readthedocs.io/en/stable/) — histogram-based and leaf-wise gradient boosting.
- [CatBoost documentation](https://catboost.ai/docs/) — ordered boosting and categorical-feature handling.
- [FAISS](https://github.com/facebookresearch/faiss) — similarity search and clustering for dense vectors.
- [Hinton and Salakhutdinov, “Reducing Dimensionality with Neural Networks”](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1127647) — autoencoder-based representation learning.
- [Redmon et al., “You Only Look Once” (YOLO)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02640) — one-stage object detection.
- [Zhang et al., “ByteTrack”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06864) — multi-object tracking with low-confidence detections.
- [Dehghani et al., “DeepSORT”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.07402) — multi-object tracking with motion and appearance cues.
- [Feichtenhofer et al., “SlowFast Networks”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.09882) — two-pathway video recognition.
- [Bertasius et al., “Is Space-Time Attention All You Need for Video Understanding?”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.17204) — spatiotemporal Transformer attention.
- [Liu et al., “Video Swin Transformer”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.13230) — hierarchical video Transformer architecture.
- [Dao et al., “FlashAttention”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14135) — IO-aware exact attention.

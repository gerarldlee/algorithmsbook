## Books

- Thomas H. Cormen, Charles E. Leiserson, Ronald L. Rivest, and Clifford Stein, *Introduction to Algorithms*. Chapters 2, 3, 15, and 16 cover divide and conquer, dynamic programming, and greedy algorithms.
- Donald E. Knuth, *The Art of Computer Programming, Volume 3: Sorting and Searching*. Detailed treatment of sorting, radix methods, and distribution counting.
- Donald E. Knuth, *The Art of Computer Programming, Volume 4A: Fundamentals of Algorithmics*. Dynamic-programming foundations and state-space analysis.
- Steven C. Van Gundy, *The Algorithm Design Manual*. Practical tradeoffs among search paradigms and implementation constraints.
- Doug Lea, *Java Concurrency in Practice*. Mutual exclusion, atomic state, thread safety, and concurrent execution.
- Steve Lavington, *The Art of Multiprocessor Programming*. Locks, concurrent data structures, and parallel programming models.
- Maurice Herlihy and Nir Shavit, *The Art of Multiprocessor Programming*. Lock-free programming, memory models, and transactional-memory foundations.

## Websites

- [Java Platform, Standard Edition API](https://docs.oracle.com/en/java/javase/21/docs/api/) — collections, `CompletableFuture`, semaphores, and atomic operations used in the chapter.
- [Rust Standard Library](https://doc.rust-lang.org/std/) — atomics, threads, mutexes, condition variables, and future interfaces.
- [Go Programming Language Specification](https://go.dev/ref/spec) and [Go Memory Model](https://go.dev/ref/mem) — goroutines, synchronization, and memory visibility.
- [TypeScript Handbook](https://www.typescriptlang.org/docs/handbook/intro.html) and [MDN SharedArrayBuffer](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/SharedArrayBuffer) — JavaScript execution and shared cross-thread memory.
- [Python free-threaded CPython documentation](https://docs.python.org/3/howto/free-threading-python.html) — global-interpreter-lock behavior and free-threaded builds.
- [Intel Intrinsics Guide](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/docs/intrinsics-guide/index.html) — explicit SIMD instructions, targets, and processor support.
- [C and C++ Core Guidelines](https://isocpp.github.io/CppCoreGuidelines/CppCoreGuidelines) and [C11 atomics reference](https://en.cppreference.com/w/c/atomic) — concurrency primitives and lock-free design considerations.
